# Swedex A2B1 – så går provet till

*How the test works.* Based on Folkuniversitetet's official papers: *Testformat för Swedex A2B1*, the A2B1 model
test (*modelltest*), the oral participant material and the FAQ. Facts from older material or from test takers
are marked **(old guide)** or **(test taker)**.

**Ditt prov / Your test:** Göteborg, **4 december 2026**, Norra Allégatan 6. Plan for a whole day (09–17).
The oral part is usually at a separate time during the day.

| Del / Part | Tid / Time | Poäng / Points | A2 | B1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Läsförståelse – reading | 60 min | 30 | 17 | **22** |
| Hörförståelse – listening | ca 30 min | 30 | 14 | **22** |
| Skriftlig uppgift – writing | 60 min | criteria | – | – |
| Muntlig uppgift – speaking | ca 15 min, alone | criteria | – | – |

- **Inga hjälpmedel** – no dictionary, no phone, nothing.
- **Ditt resultat = den svagaste delen.** *Your overall level is your lowest part.* B1 in three parts and A2 in one = A2.
- Citizenship needs **B1 in reading and listening**, and **A2 in speaking and writing**.
- Failed one part? You can redo only that part (*restprov*) – but Swedex ends **2026-12-31**.

---

## 1. Läsförståelse (reading) – 60 min, 30 p

Texter: brev, annonser, faktatexter och insändare. *Texts: letters, ads, fact texts and letters to the editor.*

| Del | Vad / What | Hur svarar man / How you answer | Model test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Brev och faktatexter | **Ja / Nej** per statement | 10 |
| **2** | Annonser | Match each **person's interest** to the right **ad** | 6 |
| **3** | Insändare (opinion letters) | **Flerval** – choose A/B/C/D | 6 |
| **4** | En faktatext | Find the sentence in the text that **means the same** (synonym sentence). Sometimes the answer is **0 = not in the text** | 8 |

**Tänk på / Watch out**
- Del 1: *men, fast, däremot* often flip the meaning. Read to the end of the sentence.
- Del 4: the words are **different** on purpose (*ledig* = *koppla av*, *lämnade* = *flyttade från*). Only choose 0 when the **idea** is missing, not when the words are missing.
- About 2 min per question. Don't stay stuck: mark it, go on, come back.

Öva: *021 Läsprov (hela)*, *038 Samma betydelse (del 4)*.

---

## 2. Hörförståelse (listening) – ca 30 min, 30 p

**Alla inspelningar spelas två gånger** – *every recording is played twice*, **directly one after the other (test taker)**.
You get **20 seconds** to read the questions before each part (30 s in del 4). Answers are written while you listen.

| Del | Vad / What | Hur svarar man / How you answer | Model test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Telefonmeddelanden – phone messages | **Flerval** (often pictures or dates), 1 question per message | 6 |
| **2** | Meddelanden och delar av radioprogram | **Write 1–5 words** | 6 |
| **3** | En dialog (+ a message) | **Write 1–5 words** | 6 |
| **4** | Radionyheter / radioinslag | Match each news item to a **headline**. **Two headlines are extra.** All items repeat after the last one | 4 |
| **5** | Intervjuer | **Sant / Falskt** – true or false | 8 |

**Tänk på / Watch out**
- Use the 20 s: read the **question word** (*var, när, vem, varför, hur mycket*) and underline it. *Var?* wants a place, not a name.
- Numbers: the first number you hear is often the trap (*spår 4 … utan spår 9*). Wait for the correction (*nej förresten, inte … utan …*).
- 1–5 words: write **one** answer. Several answers to one question can give 0 points **(old B2C1 guide)**. Small spelling errors are OK if the word is clear.
- Hardest part for most people **(test taker)**: fast speech, some background noise.

Öva: *025 Hörprov Swedex*, *028 Gammalt hörprov B1*, *027 Riktiga röster*.

## 3. Skriftlig uppgift (writing) – 60 min, 2 tasks, on paper

| Del | Uppgift / Task | Längd / Length |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Svara på ett mejl** from a friend. Answer **all their questions** (model test: 3). *Kom ihåg att börja och avsluta mejlet på ett korrekt sätt.* | short (~50 words **(test taker)**) |
| **2** | **Mejl om din vecka** to a relative/friend, with a **kalender**. "Today" is a date in the middle of the week: describe **five days** – what you **have done** (preteritum/perfekt), what you **will do** (ska / kommer att) and **what you thought** of it. Start and end correctly. | **ca 100 ord** |

**Bedömning / How it's judged** (3 criteria):
1. **Fullgörande av uppgiften / innehåll** – did you cover every point/question?
2. **Kommunikativ utformning** – does the text hang together, fit the reader, with a correct **greeting and closing**?
3. **Formell korrekthet** – grammar, word forms, spelling.

**(old guide)** If any one criterion gets 0, the whole task fails. So a missing question or a missing greeting can fail an otherwise good text.

**Tänk på / Watch out**
- Tick off each question/date in the task before you hand in.
- Friend: *Hej Emma! … Kram / Hälsningar, [namn]*. Not *MVH* to a friend.
- Calendar: check the date "today". Days before = past tense, days after = *ska*.
- Stay near the length. Much longer = more mistakes, no extra points.

Öva: *002 Skriva*, *037 Skriv: ram och former*.

## 4. Muntlig uppgift (speaking) – ca 15 min, alone with the examiner

*Du gör testet ensam. Det är ingen förberedelsetid.* No preparation time. **The test is recorded.**

| Del | Vad / What | Tid / Time |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Presentation** | Present yourself briefly, then talk with the examiner about **you and your everyday life** (work, home, free time). | ca 3 min |
| **2. Fråga varandra om en annons** | **Ad A:** you get **30 s** to read it, then the examiner **asks you** about it. **Ad B:** you swap – now **you ask** the examiner. A box gives tips (*var, hotell? pris, barn?*), but you may ask your own questions. | ca 8 min |
| **3. Prata om ett ämne** | A new topic (e.g. living in town or the country, mobile phones, travel). Talk **with** the examiner: **say what you think** and maybe tell something you've experienced. | ca 4 min |

**Bedömning / How it's judged**: production and **interaction**, communicative fluency (*kommunikativ utformning*),
vocabulary (*ordförråd*), grammar (*formell korrekthet*) and **pronunciation** (*uttal*).

**(old guide)** Scored as a whole, 40 points: interaction 18, correctness 12, pronunciation/fluency 10.
Each gets A/B/C/D. **Any D = speaking failed.** C = "the listener must ask to understand".

**Tänk på / Watch out**
- **Interaction is almost half the points.** Ask back (*Och du?*, *Vad tycker du?*), react (*Jaha, intressant!*).
- Didn't understand? Ask! *Förlåt, kan du upprepa?* / *Menar du …?* – that is good interaction, not a failure.
- Del 2: ask **full questions**: *Var ligger hotellet? Ingår frukost? Hur mycket kostar det för en familj?*
- Del 3: give an opinion **+ a reason + an example**: *Jag tycker att … eftersom … Till exempel …*
- Verb second: *På helgen **spelar jag** …*, *I Uppsala **kan man** …*

Öva: *036 Tala (Swedex)*, model answers *029–035*, collocations *039–042*.

---

## Provdagen / Test day checklist

- ID (passport or ID card), booking confirmation, pen + pencil + eraser, water, a snack (long day).
- Listening: sit where you hear well; say at once if the sound is bad.
- Order at Göteborg is given on the day **(official FAQ)**. Expect reading, listening and writing in one long block, oral separately **(test centre reports)**.
- Result certificate about **3 weeks** later.
