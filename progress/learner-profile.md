## Known weaknesses
- Date/time words (weekdays, months, i övermorgon/i förrgår, vecka forms) are slow to stick – also in English/Persian as a child. Needs spaced repetition in context.
- (002 writing) Letter frame: greeting/closing missing in task 2, wrong name in task 1, MVH to a friend; word limit exceeded (128, 164 of max 120). Noun forms (min + noun without -en; plural -er/-ar; en/ett), *att* wrongly after modal-like verbs (hinner inte att, behöver inte att), infinitive after ska (tvätt→tvätta), phonetic spelling, clear å/ä/ö dots in handwriting.
- (021) Reading del 4 synonyms: chooses '0 – ingen mening' too fast (2 tests in a row, 6 of 16 wrong). Svensk mil = 10 km (answered Nej to 'åtta mil' = 80 km).
- (013) Not improved vs 009: adjective after mitt/min/den/det must be -a (mitt nytt hem, gammlat), mitt/min by noun; irregular comparison (sämre, större, äldre, sämsta) right only after hint. Listening: förra/nu trap again; spelled kroner.
- (009) Adjective agreement en/ett/plural (gamla/nytt/kallt first try wrong; speaking *en gammalt lägenhet*, *min hem*); irregular comparison *dålig-sämre* (wrote dåligare), *bästa*; *större än* (said *en*). Listening: förra vs nuvarande.
- (011) Signal word still slow in gaps (4 of 9 wrong first try), but speaking is now mostly right. *också* + inversion in writing: *Igår åt jag också* (wrote *åt också jag*). Duration: i/för/sedan; *hade* + preteritum mix.
- **Signal word → verb form** not automatic: har/hade → supinum (sett, förstått), igår → preteritum (försökte, sa). Knows the rules (fixes after hint), slow to apply.
- Speaking: inversion after time word (*På måndag jag hade*), past days need -s (*i måndags*), *också/inte* after the verb (*jag också gick*).
- Irregular morphology: strong verb past/supine (skrave, ät), group-3 verbs (bode), comparative (stortare), o→ö plural (bokar).
- Precise vocabulary for frequent B1 words: tvungen, uppsagd, förmodligen, avboka, söka jobb, påminna/komma ihåg, däremot.
- Fixed prepositions: intresserad av, i + duration.
