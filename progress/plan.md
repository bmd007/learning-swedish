## Swedex A2B1 format (official, materials/swedex-a2b1/a2b1-testformat.pdf)
- Läsa 60 min, 30 p (A2 17, B1 22): del 1 brev/faktatext Ja/Nej · del 2 match persons ↔ ads ·
  del 3 insändare MCQ · del 4 faktatext, match synonymous sentences.
- Höra ~30 min, 30 p (A2 14, B1 22), each recording twice: del 1 messages MCQ · del 2 messages/radio,
  answer in 1–5 words · del 3 dialogue, 1–5 words · del 4 radio items ↔ headlines (2 extra) ·
  del 5 interviews Sant/Falskt.
- Skriva 60 min: (1) answer questions from an email (2) tell what you did/do/will do from calendar notes.
  Criteria: task fulfilment/content, communicative form, formal correctness.
- Tala ~15 min, 1 examiner: (1) general questions (2) ask each other questions from an ad each
  (3) conversation on a topic. Criteria: production/interaction, communicative form, vocabulary,
  correctness, pronunciation. Recorded.
- Official model test is in materials/ (gitignored, copyright: personal study only).
