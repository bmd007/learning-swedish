### 3) Your five most important mistakes

1. **"Nu jag jobbar som softwareingenjör."**
   Correct: "Nu jobbar jag som mjukvaruutvecklare."
   Rule: in Swedish the verb comes second, so after a time word like "nu" the verb comes before the subject.

2. **"Ibland jag tittar på en film."**
   Correct: "Ibland tittar jag på en film."
   Rule: same as above. After "ibland", "ofta", "sedan" and so on, put the verb first and the subject after it.

3. **"Min vänner" and "en team."**
   Correct: "mina vänner" and "ett lag."
   Rule: possessives and articles must match the noun. Plural nouns need "mina", and you need to learn whether each word is en or ett, so learn nouns together with their article.

4. **"Bästa del av min jobb."**
   Correct: "Den bästa delen av mitt jobb."
   Rule: with a superlative, use "den bästa" plus the definite noun ending, and "jobb" is an ett-word, so it is "mitt jobb".

5. **"Jag har flyttat till Sverige i åtta år" and "Jag bor på en stad."**
   Correct: "Jag har bott i Sverige i åtta år" and "Jag bor i en stad."
   Rule: use "bo i" for where you live. "Flytta" describes the move itself, so it doesn't fit with "i åtta år". Also note: "eftersom" starts a subordinate clause, so "eftersom många människor har jobb" is fine, but the negation or adverb goes before the verb in these clauses, as in "eftersom det inte finns".

### 4) Five useful phrases

- "Kan du upprepa det, tack?" for when you need to hear something again.
- "Vad menar du med det?" to ask for clarification.
- "Det beror på, eftersom..." to give a reason in a natural way.
- "Enligt min åsikt är det bättre att..." to state your opinion in the topic part.
- "Jag håller delvis med dig, men..." to politely disagree and defend your view.

Overall, this was a solid A2 performance with some B1 strengths in interaction. If you would like, we can practise one of the parts again, or work on any of these mistakes.
