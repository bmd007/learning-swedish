## Problem 1: Irregular word forms (verbs, comparatives, plurals)

You wrote *skrave, ät, bode, stortare, bokar*. You know the forms change; you just don't know how yet.

**Persian hook:** in Persian you learn two stems for each verb, for example رفتن → رو / رفت and دیدن → بین / دید. Nobody can predict رفت from رو; you just learned it. Swedish strong verbs are the same idea, **but they come in vowel groups**. Learn the group and you know many verbs at once:

| Group (vowels) | Examples (infinitiv – preteritum – supinum) |
|---|---|
| **i – e – i** | skriva – **skrev** – skrivit · bli – blev – blivit · bita – bet – bitit |
| **i – a – u** | dricka – drack – druckit · springa – sprang – sprungit · sitta – satt – suttit · hitta is NOT strong (hittade) |
| **y/ju – ö – u** | flyga – flög – flugit · bjuda – bjöd – bjudit · frysa – frös – frusit |
| **a – o – a** | ta – tog – tagit · fara – for – farit · dra – drog – dragit |
| **ä – å/a – ä/u** | äta – **åt** – **ätit** · ligga – låg – legat · se – såg – sett |

- **Supinum of strong verbs always ends in -it:** ätit, skrivit, druckit. *ät* is never a form.
- **Short verbs** (group 3) get **-dde / -tt**: bo – **bodde** – bott, tro – trodde – trott. The double *d* shows a short vowel.
- **Comparatives:** normally *-are* (snabb – snabbare). A small group changes the vowel to ö/ä/y: **stor – större – störst**, lång – längre, ung – yngre, gammal – äldre. Some are totally irregular: bra – bättre – bäst, liten – mindre – minst.
- **Plural with o → ö:** en bok – **böcker**, en fot – fötter, en son – söner.

## Problem 3: Prepositions as fixed chunks
- intresserad **av** (not på)
- Hon har bott här **i** fem år (how long) · för fem år **sedan** (ago)
- tvätta **sig** / tvätta **händerna**. *tvätta sig om händerna* is not Swedish.

**Tip:** learn verb/adjective + preposition as **one word**: *intresserad-av*, *tänka-på*, *vänta-på*, *bero-på*.
